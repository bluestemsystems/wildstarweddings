What it feels like to work with me

I pay attention.

Not just to what you say, but how you say it. The pauses, the tone, the things you almost skip over. That’s where the real story is.

On your wedding day, I’m not the main attraction. I’m there to guide the moment. To keep things steady. To make sure you can actually be present, instead of worrying about what comes next.

  • I’ll help shape a ceremony that sounds like you, not a template
  • I’ll hold the pacing so nothing feels rushed or dragged out
  • I’ll adjust in real time, because no ceremony ever unfolds exactly the same way
  • I’ll make space for emotion without making it uncomfortable

So when you’re standing there, you don’t have to think about anything else. You just get to be there, together.
