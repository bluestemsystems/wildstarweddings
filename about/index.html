That’s me. I clean up nicely for ceremonies, I promise.

I didn’t plan to become a wedding officiant.

A friend asked me to marry her, and I said yes. Mostly because I loved her and wanted to be part of the day. What I didn’t expect was how much I’d love the work itself. The preparation, the conversations, the moment right before the ceremony starts when everything gets quiet and real. I’ve been doing it ever since.

I’m Wren. I’m based in New Braunfels, in the Texas Hill Country. I have a background in design and a long history of caring too much about words. Both of those things show up in how I approach ceremonies.

I write every ceremony from scratch. I want to know your story, your people, the shape of your relationship, so what comes out sounds like you, not like a wedding.

These days, I’m not the only one standing up front. I’ve brought on a small team of officiants I trust to deliver those ceremonies, so Wildstar can be there for more couples, on more days. The writing is still mine, every time. That part doesn’t change.

If you want something real, I’d love to help you make it.
